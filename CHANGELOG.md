# Changelog & Feedback Log

This document records the iterations and improvements made to the project based on feedback from five different reviewers.

---

## 👤 User Feedback #1: Navigation and Clarity
- **Task:** General exploration of the site to explain its purpose.
- **Identified Issues:** Broken links and default values found in several sections.
- **Reviewer Quote:** 
  > "A ver, se entiende lo que quiere hacer pero esta horrible y no funciona bien"
- **Changes Applied:** Updated link names for clarity and improved navigation to ensure users can orient themselves easily.

---

## 👤 User Feedback #2: Style and Originality
- **Task:** Reviewing the overall visual style of the site.
- **Identified Issues:** The design felt unoriginal and derivative of another existing site.
- **Reviewer Quote:** 
  > "Si es que otra persona ve se va a dar cuenta de que es una copia de otro sitio y para esa persona le va a bajar puntos"
- **Changes Applied:** Shifted to a more generalized and unique style to reduce the influence of other sites and establish a more distinct brand.

---

## 👤 User Feedback #3: Responsive Design and Readability
- **Task:** Identification of issues within specific site sections.
- **Identified Issues:** Small text on large monitors and poor mobile experience in the second section of the home page.
- **Reviewer Quote:** 
  > "Aca el texto no se ve nada, es demasiado pequeño y esta muy vacio y en telefono es horrible"
- **Changes Applied:** Implemented full responsiveness for both large screens and mobile devices, ensuring text scaling and layout adjustments are handled correctly.

---

## 👤 User Feedback #4: Layout and Visual Density
- **Task:** Gathering suggestions for general site improvements.
- **Identified Issues:** The introductory section of the home page felt too empty.
- **Reviewer Quote:** 
  > "Añadile unas lineas para que destaque mas o algo, se ve muy vacio"
- **Changes Applied:** Added a red border to the section and a red separator line for the final section to improve visual interest and structure.

---

## 👤 User Feedback #5: Professional Polish and Consistency
- **Task:** Researching inspiration from professional websites.
- **Identified Issues:** Lacking the "professional finish" typical of high-end platforms like Twitter.
- **Reviewer Quote:** 
  > "Twitter es una pagina bien hecha, todo funciona bien y se ve limpio"
- **Changes Applied:** Focused on styling consistency and reducing "amateur" elements to provide a cleaner, more professional user experience.
